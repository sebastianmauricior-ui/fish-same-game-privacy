Privacy Policy - Fish Same Game

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fish Same Game handles information when users play the game.

Application: Fish Same Game
Developer: Sebastian Mauricio Regalado
Contact: yukushigames@gmail.com

1. Information We May Collect

Fish Same Game may process certain information necessary to provide the game's features, display advertising, maintain the operation of the game, and provide the online leaderboard.

Depending on the features used and the third-party services integrated into the game, information that may be processed includes:

  • Technical or player identifiers.
  • The name used within the game.
  • Scores submitted to the leaderboard.
  • Device information.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Information related to interaction with advertisements.
  • Technical and diagnostic information.

Fish Same Game does not directly request passwords, credit card numbers, banking information, personal contacts, or precise location.

2. Data Stored Locally

Fish Same Game may store information locally on the user's device to preserve game progress.

This information may include:

  • Score.
  • In-game coins.
  • Game progress.
  • The state of fish inside the aquarium.
  • Game settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide the game's features and preserve the player's progress.

3. Online Leaderboard

Fish Same Game uses LootLocker to provide online game features, including the leaderboard system.

When the player uses these features, information necessary to identify the player within the service and record the player's score may be processed.

This may include:

  • A player identifier.
  • The name used within the game.
  • The score submitted to the leaderboard.
  • Technical information necessary to provide the service.

LootLocker provides services for managing players and leaderboards. Information processed by LootLocker is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

4. Advertising

Fish Same Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The application may display:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Interstitial advertisements.
  • Rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements may provide in-game rewards after the user completes the required advertisement.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information related to the device, advertising identifiers, interaction with advertisements, and other information necessary to provide and measure advertising services.

5. Third-Party Services

Fish Same Game uses third-party services for certain features.

The main third-party services used by the game include:

  • Google AdMob - used to provide advertisements within the game.
  • LootLocker - used to provide the online leaderboard and related player functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. How We Use Information

The information processed may be used to:

  • Save and restore the player's progress.
  • Provide game functionality.
  • Record scores on the online leaderboard.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Provide rewarded advertisements.
  • Maintain and improve the game.
  • Detect errors and technical problems.
  • Maintain the security and stability of the application.
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

7. Sharing of Information

The developer does not directly sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary for Fish Same Game to operate, including Google and its advertising services and LootLocker and its player and leaderboard services.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Fish Same Game.

Information stored locally remains on the user's device unless a particular feature requires information to be sent to an online service.

Third-party services used by the application maintain their own security mechanisms and privacy practices.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by Fish Same Game may be deleted by uninstalling the application or by using applicable reset functionality within the game.

Information processed by third-party services, including LootLocker and Google, is subject to the respective providers' data retention and deletion policies.

If you have a request regarding information associated with Fish Same Game, you may contact the developer using the email address provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Fish Same Game is not specifically designed exclusively for children.

The application does not directly request sensitive personal information such as passwords, banking information, or precise location.

Data processing and advertising practices will comply with applicable Google Play requirements and the requirements of the third-party services used by the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to Fish Same Game, the services used by the application, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy will be updated.
